    Why the two lens views differ

    The camera sends one stream. Both lenses arrive stacked in a single frame: the upper half is one lens, the lower half the other. The app crops one half when you ask for a single lens, so switching views costs no second connection.

    One lens is motorized, the other is not. The moving lens can pan and tilt from the app. The fixed lens cannot move on its own; it is aimed by turning the housing by hand. A fixed lens that refuses to pan is the hardware, not a fault.

    Which half is the moving lens comes from the device capabilities. While the server does not report them, the views are labelled by position, not by role.
